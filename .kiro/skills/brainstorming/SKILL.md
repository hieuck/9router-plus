# Brainstorming

Find out what your human partner actually wants and why, before building anything.

## First: What Do You Actually Know?

- **Everything that matters is in the request** (clear, quick task) → do it
- **Something non-trivial is missing** → start the conversation

Before implementing, in order:
1. Lay out the approaches and let partner pick one
2. Write the design doc (short or full)
3. Get their approval
4. Then plan and implement

## Look Before You Ask

In an existing project, read what they referred to and related code. Never ask what the project already answers or reopen what's already decided.

## Your First Message

- Echo request back in your own words (what, who, why)
- Add what you found that bears on it (how relevant parts work, what change would touch)
- Flag anything they may not have considered (constraint, conflict, cheaper path)
- One open question about the biggest thing left unsettled — say why you're asking

## Spike to Feel Things Out

When approach is unclear: time-boxed prototype (agreed duration), no production polish, throw away after learning. Keeps brainstorming light.
